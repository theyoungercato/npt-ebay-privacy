Privacy Policy

Northern Plains Trading Co Inventory Sync

Effective: October 26, 2026

This privacy policy describes an internal software integration used by Northern Plains Trading Co to help manage its own eBay inventory and sales records.

Information processed

With the seller's authorization, the integration may access listing and inventory identifiers, SKUs, item descriptions, prices, quantities, shipping information, sales status, and transaction-level financial totals. The intended inventory workflow excludes buyer names, addresses, and contact details. Authentication tokens needed to connect to eBay are used only for the authorized integration.

How information is used

Information is used to synchronize the business's eBay listings with its private inventory spreadsheet, reconcile sales and direct costs, and prepare operational reports. The integration is not intended for advertising or resale of personal information.

Storage and service providers

Processing may involve eBay, Cloudflare, and Google services. The integration uses access-controlled configuration for credentials, HTTPS for data in transit, and a private Google Sheets workbook for inventory records. The eBay account deletion notification endpoint maintains minimal technical receipts to verify processing.

Retention and deletion

Business inventory and accounting data is retained as necessary for recordkeeping. Credentials can be revoked through eBay authorization controls. The integration is designed not to store buyer-identifying details in the seller inventory sheet. Applicable deletion notifications are handled under the integration's eBay account deletion procedure.

Data sharing

Information is not sold to third parties. Information is shared only with the service providers required to operate the authorized inventory workflow, or as required by law.

Questions

Contact Northern Plains Trading Co using the business's eBay seller messaging channel for privacy questions or requests.
